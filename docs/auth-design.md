# Screeno authentication design

The split illustration/form layout follows the user-supplied reference. Screeno's cobalt blue and lime remain the brand colors. Manrope remains the body and interface face; the locally hosted Barlow Condensed ExtraBold is used for auth headings, the landing hero, and the final landing call to action. Its SIL Open Font License is bundled in `public/fonts/barlow-condensed/OFL.txt`.

Recovery inspiration: [Imran Jakir's password recovery and verification flow](https://dribbble.com/shots/26822066-Forgot-Password-Flow-UI-Modern-Verification-Reset-Screens). The implementation uses original line-art ornaments, progress steps, and existing demo verification behavior; no artwork was copied from that reference.

## Generated illustration

### Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original premium editorial 3D cartoon illustration for Screeno, a recruiter hiring workspace. Portrait 3:4 composition used as left half of sign-in screen. A friendly expressive STICK FIGURE character, round white head with two large charcoal eyes, minimal thin dark navy stick arms and legs, simple cobalt blue rounded torso, lime green little neck scarf, stands on a smooth cobalt blue platform holding a large lime green candidate folder with a simple checkmark. It should unmistakably be a stylish stick man cartoon, not a furry creature, not a realistic human. Charming handcrafted clay and smooth matte vinyl rendering, soft bright studio daylight, subtle paper texture, confident welcoming pose. Background: dreamy airy pale blue white cloudlike sculptural shapes with one abstract archway, small floating blank profile tiles and lime accents conveying finding great people. Strong cobalt #0747BD and electric lime #AEFF40 brand colors; soft ivory and pale icy blue. Character centered in upper-middle portion, substantial uncluttered dark cobalt negative space in bottom 24 percent for HTML headline overlay. Sophisticated clean art direction, sculptural depth and soft shadows. No words, letters, logos, watermarks or readable text.
